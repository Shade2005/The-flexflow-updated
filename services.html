03

Common Questions

How the disciplines and sprints actually run.

Have a specific question not covered below? Bring it to the 30-minute call or send a direct email.

Ask a direct question →
Can I bundle multiple disciplines into a single sprint?

Yes. In fact, most clients combine complementary disciplines. The two most common combinations are Web Design + AI Visibility (so your new high-speed site launches with validated Knowledge Graph schemas) and Brand Identity + 30-Day Social Kit (so your team has all initial collateral designed immediately). During the first scoping call, we sequence them so you never pay for redundant work.

How are projects structured and delivered?

Projects are delivered as focused, milestone-driven sprints with clear delivery roadmaps, weekly async progress updates, and direct founder communication. Every phase has specific deliverables agreed upon before work begins.

Who actually builds the site, writes the copy, and manages the ads?

One practitioner: Hamza, the founder of The FlexFlow. You are not handed off to a junior account manager, a junior designer, or an outsourced subcontractor. The person you speak with on the scoping call is the exact person who designs in Figma, writes the HTML/CSS/JS, injects the schemas, and optimizes the ad bids.

What happens to our code, files, and ad accounts if we stop working together?

You own 100% of everything from day one. All GitHub repositories, cloud hosting accounts, Figma source files, Google Analytics properties, and Meta Ads Manager accounts are set up under your company's name. There are zero proprietary software lock-ins, zero exit penalties, and no ongoing licensing fees.

How do you track performance and report results?

You get live dashboard access and transparent weekly summaries covering key metrics—such as lead volume, cost per lead, keyword movement, and Core Web Vitals performance—with direct access to your native ad and analytics platforms.

How quickly can our sprint or campaign kick off?

Because we cap active client capacity at three concurrent builds, sprint onboarding depends on current slot availability (shown in the live status box at the top of this page). Typically, a new sprint kicks off within 5 to 10 business days following the initial scoping call and proposal sign-off.
