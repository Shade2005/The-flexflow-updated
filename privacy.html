Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026. This policy describes how The FlexFlow (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, protects, and handles information across our website and client engagements.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit theflexflow.in, book an introductory call, or engage our marketing engineering services, we collect information necessary to facilitate direct communication and deliver custom digital solutions:

  • Contact Information: Full name, professional email address, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, and current website URL submitted via our contact forms or scheduling calendar.
  • Project Scoping Telemetry: Business objectives, marketing budget parameters, current traffic metrics, and target customer profiles voluntarily shared during scoping conversations.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized server logs, browser type, device characteristics, and page engagement timestamps used solely to maintain sub-second performance and security integrity.

2. How We Use Your Information

All data collected is utilized strictly within the scope of founder-led services:

  • To evaluate project feasibility and prepare transparent, fixed-scope sprint roadmaps.
  • To directly correspond regarding bookings, design deliverables, and campaign performance reviews.
  • To prevent malicious traffic, enforce security headers, and maintain infrastructure uptime.
  • Zero Data Brokerage: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your contact details or business metrics to third-party ad networks or brokers under any circumstances.

3. Client Confidentiality & Proprietary Assets

Because we work with venture-backed SaaS startups, architectural brands, and direct-to-consumer businesses, proprietary discretion is paramount:

  • Non-Disclosure: Proprietary client funnels, unpublished conversion copy, Figma prototypes, and CRM integrations are treated as confidential intellectual property.
  • Public Case Studies: We publish client metrics and campaign breakdowns only with explicit, written founder authorization.

4. Analytics & Third-Party Services

Our website uses lightweight, privacy-conscious utilities:

  • CDN & Hosting: Deployed via Vercel edge network with strict HTTPS encryption and automated security headers.
  • External Integrations: If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp or schedule a call via calendar integrations, those services operate under their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Rights & Inquiries

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of any personal information we retain. To exercise these rights or inquire about this policy, contact our founder directly:
