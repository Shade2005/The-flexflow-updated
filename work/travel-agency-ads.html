Voyage India Travel: 160+ Holiday Booking Leads at ₹14.54 CPL

Voyage India Travel partnered with The FlexFlow to compete against national online travel aggregators (OTAs) on an initial paid social budget of just ₹2,542.35. By replacing generic destination awareness ads with circuit-specific Meta Ads routed directly into structured WhatsApp booking triage, the campaign generated 160+ qualified holiday package inquiries across Kashmir, Delhi–Agra, and Hyderabad at an average cost per lead (CPL) of ₹14.54 — roughly 22× cheaper than typical Indian travel OTA acquisition benchmarks.

Verified Performance by Travel Circuit

All numbers below were pulled directly from the client's Meta Ads Manager account and matched against incoming WhatsApp package inquiries:

Holiday Circuit Qualified Leads Cost Per Lead (CPL) Conversion Path
Kashmir Circuit 64 leads ₹13.80 Click-to-WhatsApp pre-filled itinerary prompt
Delhi–Agra Golden Triangle 58 leads ₹14.20 Click-to-WhatsApp pre-filled itinerary prompt
Hyderabad Heritage 38 leads ₹15.60 Click-to-WhatsApp pre-filled itinerary prompt
Total Campaign 160+ leads ₹14.54 blended ₹2,542.35 total ad spend

How the Funnel Was Engineered

  • Circuit-Isolated Ad Sets: Instead of bidding on broad "India holiday" keywords against billion-dollar OTAs, creative and targeting were segmented into three specific routes with transparent starting package cues.
  • Zero-Friction WhatsApp Handoff: Eliminated slow multi-page lead forms in favor of Click-to-WhatsApp threads pre-populated with travel dates, passenger count, and route interest.
  • Sub-60-Second Triage: Automated initial itinerary replies allowed the sales team to quote qualified travelers immediately while intent was highest.
