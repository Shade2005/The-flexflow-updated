Performance Marketing Case Study · Local Wellness

Zara Salon & Spa: 1.08M City Impressions at ₹2.35 CPM

Zara Salon & Spa struggled with local discovery outside walk-in foot traffic. The FlexFlow engineered a 5km South Delhi hyperlocal geofenced Meta Ads campaign pairing treatment-specific creative hooks with direct slot-booking handoffs. The campaign delivered 1.08M+ local impressions at an ultra-efficient ₹2.35 CPM and generated 28+ confirmed high-ticket booking inquiries at ₹45.00 per lead, filling their appointment calendar through paid social alone.

Verified Campaign Telemetry

Metric Measured Result Operational Context
Total Local Reach 1.08M+ impressions 5km radius geofence around South Delhi location
Cost Per 1,000 Impressions (CPM) ₹2.35 Achieved via high-retention native reel & offer creatives

Cost Per Booking Inquiry ₹45.00 Direct-to-booking qualification flow

How the Hyperlocal Engine Worked

  • 5km Pin-Drop Geofencing: Eliminated wasted city-wide impressions by locking delivery strictly to commuters and residents within realistic travel radius of the studio.
  • High-Ticket Service Framing: Positioned bridal and spa packages around concrete treatment outcomes rather than steep discounting that attracts low-retention shoppers.
  • Direct Slot Confirmation: Routed ad clicks straight to live appointment scheduling so inquiries converted into booked calendar slots on the same day.
